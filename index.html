How to comment

Anyone can comment on a planning application. You do not have to live next to the site, and you do not need to be an expert.

What to do

  1. Open the planning portal page for the application you want to comment on, using the links above.
  2. Find the option to comment on the application. It is usually labelled “Comment”, “Make a comment” or “Have your say”.
  3. Give your name and address. Comments submitted anonymously are normally disregarded.
  4. Say clearly that you object, and give your reasons in your own words.
  5. Submit it, and keep a copy of the confirmation.
  6. If you want to object to both applications, repeat this for the second one.

What makes a comment count

The council can only take account of certain kinds of objection, known as material planning considerations. These include things like traffic and road safety, loss of light or privacy, noise, flooding and drainage, the scale and appearance of the buildings, and the loss of trees or green space.

The council cannot take account of things like the effect on property prices, who the developer is, or a boundary dispute with a neighbour. Keeping to the considerations above makes a comment much harder to set aside.
