How to comment

Anyone can comment on a planning application. You do not have to live next to the site, and you do not need to be an expert.

However, you do need to provide valid reasons for your objection. "I don't like it" will not hold much weight with the Wiltshire Council Planning department.

Commenting on the Wiltshire Council Planning Portal

  1. Open the planning portal page for the application you want to comment on, using the links above.
  2. Click on the Comments tab, followed by the Comment button.
  3. Fill in the form, giving your name, email and address. Comments submitted anonymously will be disregarded.
  4. For Commentator Type you should select one of the following options:

Commenting by Email

You can also comment by email, by sending your comment to developmentmanagement@wiltshire.gov.uk.

The title of the email should be "Objection to <Planning application reference>" (e.g. "Objection to PL/2026/04847)"

In the body of the email, make sure you include the following:

  • Your name
  • Your address
  • State clearly that you object to the application
  • Your reasons for objecting

Failing to provide any of these may result in your comment being disregarded.

Commenting by Post

You can also comment by post, by sending your comment to the any of following addresses:

  • Wiltshire Council, Bythesea Road, Trowbridge, Wiltshire BA14 8JN
  • Monkton Park offices, Monkton Hill, Chippenham, Wiltshire SN15 1ER
  • Bourne Hill, Salisbury, Wiltshire, SP1 3UZ

In your letter, make sure you include the following:

  • Your name
  • Your address
  • State clearly that you object to the application
  • Your reasons for objecting

Failing to provide any of these may result in your comment being disregarded.

Comments close on October 1st 2026!

Late comments may not be considered. If you intend to comment, do it now.
